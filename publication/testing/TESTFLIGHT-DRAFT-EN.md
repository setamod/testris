## Beta description
Popzuno is an offline swipe-and-match puzzle game with optional photos of your people. Five modes offer stages, rising rows, timed scoring and peaceful Relax play. Match shapes to earn Bomb, Rocket, Shuffle, Cross and Nova. Photos and records stay on your device. No ads, accounts or in-app purchases. Daily 90 is a local daily challenge, without online rankings.

## Review notes
No login required. Home → Play opens five mode choices. Select a mode and tap the start button. Home → Your people opens optional local photo customization; bundled illustrations work without photos. Home → How to play contains controls, powers and mode descriptions. Settings contains privacy/licenses and support contact. Gameplay works offline. No advertising, analytics SDK, purchases or online user content are included. Records are local. Background interruptions pause gameplay; the player resumes explicitly.

# Popzuno — Privacy policy

## Scope and contact

Offline mobile and portable versions of Popzuno. Developer: Mert Bekisoglu. Privacy and support: mertbek@gmail.com. Updated 2 November 2026.

## Data processed on your device

Optional character names, resized/cropped copies of selected photos, framing settings, records, stages, language and audio/haptic preferences are stored in the app’s local storage. Photos are optional; default illustrated characters are available.

## Photos and face detection

Only the photo you select is processed. Face location and cropping run on your device; there is no identifying face template, face-recognition account or photo upload. Use photos you have permission to use, including the necessary permission for other people and children. The camera is used only if you choose it through the iOS system picker. Android uses a system picker for individual images without broad gallery permission.

## Optional advertising

The base game works offline. Google AdMob and UMP are contacted only when a player in the adult age band asks to prepare optional ads in Settings. Ads are never compulsory. Personalization and advertising-ID access are disabled. The ad provider may process IP address, device/app/network information, diagnostics, performance and ad interactions. Photos, face measurements and character names are not passed to ad SDKs. Manage ad privacy choices in Settings.

## Retention, deletion and backups

Local data remains until you delete it. Your people removes individual photos; Delete all game data also removes names, records and preferences. Original gallery images are not changed. Depending on your settings, iOS app data may enter operating-system backups, which the app cannot delete retrospectively. Android app backup is disabled. Portable-version storage is managed by your browser. Manage system/browser backups in their settings. Support emails are retained as needed to handle the request; contact us to request deletion.

## External pages and changes

Opening support/privacy pages or an email app involves those services’ own connection, security and privacy practices. Game photos are not automatically sent to those pages. Changes to data handling require updated policy and store disclosures before release.

## One-time Premium

Download, all five modes, standard photos and powers are free. Premium contains cosmetics only, with no subscription or winning advantage. Apple/Google handle payment details; we do not receive card data. Entitlements come from verified store transactions. Restore is available; refunds/revocations remove access when updated store information arrives. Transaction records may be stored locally.

## Children and local limits

The game serves a mixed audience including children. Only a coarse age band is stored on-device, not a full birth date. Children, teens and unknown ages do not start ads or new purchases. Rescues are limited to three per rolling 24 hours on this device using uptime. Quota and store rights are separate from game-data deletion, which does not cancel store purchases. Reinstall/multiple-device player limits cannot be guaranteed.
